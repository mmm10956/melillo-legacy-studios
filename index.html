About the Author

Matthew Melillo

“Stories about loyalty, survival, sacrifice, and the people worth fighting for.”

Matthew Melillo writes stories in which ordinary lives collide with extraordinary danger. His work moves from military action and eerie mystery to centuries-spanning adventure and historical intrigue. He is drawn to characters who face impossible choices and find the strength to keep moving forward.
