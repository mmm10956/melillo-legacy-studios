About the Author

Matthew Melillo

“Stories about loyalty, survival, sacrifice, and the people worth fighting for.”

Matthew Melillo is an author, storyteller, and founder of Melillo Legacy Studios. His work spans military thrillers, horror, historical fantasy, manga, and anime, creating character-driven stories where ordinary lives collide with extraordinary danger. His published works include Caston’s Revenge, The Unknown, A River Through Time, and The Last Order of the Templar. Through Melillo Legacy Studios, he continues expanding these worlds into new novels, manga, and animated projects.
